Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026

This policy explains how Nearlio collects, uses, discloses, stores and protects information when you use the Nearlio mobile application and related services.

Operator and contact

Nearlio is operated by Khagendra Thapa in New South Wales, Australia. Privacy questions, access or correction requests, complaints, and deletion requests can be sent to nearlio.support@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, Nearlio may collect:

  • Account and identity information: email address, authentication provider, name, account identifiers, and email-verification status.
  • Profile information: profile photo and information you choose to provide, such as city and country.
  • Marketplace content: listings, descriptions, prices, categories, listing locations, photos, saved listings and saved searches.
  • Communications and safety information: messages, conversation metadata, reports, blocked-user records and related moderation information.
  • Job information: job listings, company and role details, application answers, cover letters, application status, and résumés or CVs uploaded by applicants.
  • Notification and technical information: push-notification tokens, device or app identifiers, timestamps, diagnostic information and security logs needed to operate and protect the service.
  • Plan and promotion information: membership or listing-boost status when these features are available. Nearlio does not currently process payment-card details in the app.

Nearlio may handle general location information that you enter, such as a city, country or listing location. Nearlio does not currently require continuous background tracking of your precise location.

2. Device permissions

Nearlio may request access to photos, camera, files or notifications when needed for a feature you choose to use—for example, uploading a listing image or résumé, or receiving message alerts. You can manage permissions in Android settings, although disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

3. How we use information

  • create, secure and manage accounts;
  • publish and display listings and profiles;
  • provide chat, saved-search, notification and safety features;
  • allow applicants to apply for jobs and employers to review applications;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, spam and security incidents;
  • respond to support, privacy and legal requests;
  • maintain, troubleshoot and improve Nearlio; and
  • comply with applicable laws and enforce our Terms.

4. Who can see or receive information

  • Other users: information intentionally published in a profile or listing may be visible to Nearlio users. Messages are visible to conversation participants.
  • Employers: when you apply for a job, the job owner can access the application information and résumé you submit for that application.
  • Service providers: Nearlio uses providers such as Supabase for authentication, databases, file storage and server functions; Google for Google Sign-In; and Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications. These providers process data to deliver their services under their own security and privacy terms.
  • Legal and safety disclosures: information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate abuse, or respond to lawful requests.
  • Business changes: information may be transferred as part of a genuine sale, merger, restructuring or transfer of Nearlio, subject to appropriate protections.

Nearlio does not sell personal information. Your information may be processed or stored outside Australia because cloud providers operate internationally.

5. Résumé privacy

Résumés are intended to be private. Access is limited through application controls to the applicant and the owner of the job to which the résumé was submitted. Applicants should avoid including information that is not necessary for the job application, such as passport numbers, banking details or government identity-document numbers.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain information while your account is active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Nearlio, protect users, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. You can delete your account in the app. You can also use the public instructions on the account-deletion page.

Account deletion is designed to remove the account and associated profile data, listings, listing files and private job-application files. Limited information may remain temporarily in protected backups, security logs, or where retention is reasonably required by law, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or safety obligations. Information that has been irreversibly de-identified is not personal information and may be retained.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including authentication, access controls and database or storage rules. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password private, use a secure email account, and report suspected unauthorised access promptly.

8. Access, correction and complaints

You can update certain information in the app. To request access to or correction of other personal information, or to make a privacy complaint, email nearlio.support@gmail.com. Describe your request without sending passwords, verification codes or unnecessary identity documents. We may need to verify that you control the relevant account. We will review and respond within a reasonable period.

9. Children

Nearlio is not designed for children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Nearlio changes or legal requirements evolve. The effective date at the top will be updated, and material changes may also be communicated in the app or through another appropriate channel.
