Delete your Nearlio account

You can permanently delete your account and associated data directly in Nearlio. If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by email.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to Nearlio.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Read the warning and confirm deletion.

Deletion is permanent. Do not use this option for an account you intend to keep.

If you cannot sign in

Email the Nearlio support address from the email account associated with your Nearlio account. Use the subject “Nearlio account deletion request” and state that you want the account deleted.

Request deletion by email

Do not send your password, one-time code, résumé, payment information or identity documents. We may ask you to complete a reasonable account-ownership check. Requests are normally processed within 30 days.

Data deleted with the account

Account deletion is designed to remove the account and associated profile information, listings, listing images, saved items or searches, push tokens, reports or blocks, job applications, cover letters and private résumé files controlled by Nearlio.

Limited retention

Limited records may remain temporarily in protected backups or security logs, or be retained where reasonably necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or user safety. Data that has been irreversibly de-identified may be retained because it can no longer identify you.

Delete particular content without deleting your account

Where available, you can edit or delete your own listings, saved searches and other content from within Nearlio. For help with another data request, email nearlio.support@gmail.com.
